Engineering

Four chapters of the build.

CH·1

Compressed reality

The 27 MB source GLB is Draco-compressed and its 49 textures re-encoded to WebP — 4.5 MB on the wire, loaded once with real progress reporting.

CH·2

Studio lighting

PMREM environment, key/rim/fill directional lights, hemisphere, contact shadow and drifting dust — all relit when you switch the theme.

CH·3

Live materials

Paint, rims and calipers are mapped to the model's real material slots (E34_CAR_PAINT, RIM_CHROME, CAR_PinzaFreni) and transition with a colour lerp — no model reloads.

CH·4

Procedural roof skin

The carbon and panorama roofs are generated at runtime: the roof surface is raycast from above and re-skinned with a procedural carbon weave or dark glass — a real change on the real model.
